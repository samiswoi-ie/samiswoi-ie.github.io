Retail Store Manager / Supervisor

Supervise and assist with day-to-day operations of the store to ensure a high level of customer satisfaction.

Pay

From €41,000.00 per year

Job Type

Full Time

Work Location

In Person

Responsibilities

  • Manage and motivate a team. Organize and delegate work
  • Ensure customer service is up to the highest standards
  • Monitor store sales
  • Control stock levels. Manage re-orders, shelf refills, expiration dates, and write-offs
  • Ensure the store is clean and well-presented at all times

  • Create work schedules for employees
